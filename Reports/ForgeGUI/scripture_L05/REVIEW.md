## Assessment

**Strengths:** visually a strong, prestigious jade pagoda. It's clearly taller than Levels 3 and 4 and has clean geometry.

**Mandatory failures:** it has **four storeys and four roof tiers, not three**. It's also much narrower than Level 4, so the Stage I footprint shrinks at L5 instead of holding.

**Consequence for Level 6:** a four-tier L5 would take away part of Levels 6–7, whose approved specs are three- and four-storey buildings.

No retries were made. Nothing was integrated, committed, or changed in server data, footprints or saves.
