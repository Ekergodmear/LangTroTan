# Level 6 VFX anchor correction (proposal, not applied)

The Level 6 v2 VFX anchors (`Assets/ThirdParty/ForgeGUI/_Candidates/ScripturePavilion_L06_v2/VFX_ANCHORS_UNITY.json`, generated by `Tools/ForgeGUI/l6_terrace_detail.py`) give the upper tier one height, 0.94. A QA probe of the actual model shows the upper tier is stepped:

| Surface | Anchor | Measured (runs 9, 10) | Proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower landing (`tier1`) | 0.47 | 0.472 | unchanged |
| Upper tier front strip | 0.94 | 0.939 | keep as `tier2` top |
| Upper tier raised middle / back terraces | 0.94 | 1.178 / 1.195 | add `tier2_raised_top = 1.19` |

Measurement: `StageSiteCapture.RunL6SpiritV5` adds temporary in-memory MeshColliders to the QA model, raycasts down at fixed points and removes them (nothing saved). Machine-readable values: `anchor_correction_proposal.json`.

Effect today: flat effects placed at 0.94 sit under the raised terraces. This hid the v4 release pulse and the first v5 waves, and lets the clipping check under-count particles inside the raised terraces.

Affected systems: SonMonSpiritGathering (v3), SonMonSpiritGatheringV4 and V5/V6 (`Configure`, `SurfaceY`, release heights), the v4/v5 capture clipping check, and `BuildL6SpiritQAPlayer` (bakes anchors into the QA player scene).

Not done, pending review: the ForgeGUI anchor file and its generator are unchanged. Adopting this needs (1) a finer probe line to locate the step between the front strip and the raised terraces, (2) the new field added through the generator so provenance stays reproducible, and (3) the consumers above reading it. Until then V5/V6 QA uses the measured values through `SetSurfaceHeights` only.
